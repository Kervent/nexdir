# Code of Conduct

Nexdir follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This applies to GitHub, the Nexdir Discord server and every other project space.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Nexdir to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intentions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, malicious links or deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers privately (Discord moderators or a private message to
the project owner on GitHub). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
content, warn, temporarily or permanently ban anyone who violates this code.

Depending on the impact, the usual steps are:

1. **Correction** — a private, friendly note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a clear warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction in project spaces for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated violations, harassment or threats.

Moderation decisions on Discord are recorded in the private #mod-logs channel. Team members
who are involved in a report do not take part in deciding it.
