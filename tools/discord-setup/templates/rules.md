# Community rules

By taking part in this server you agree to these rules and to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

**1. Be respectful.** Treat everyone kindly. Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, discrimination or personal attacks.

**2. No harassment.** No threats, stalking, unwanted DMs or pile-ons.

**3. No spam or self-promotion.** No flooding, chain messages, unsolicited ads or invite links.

**4. No malicious links or files.** Never share malware, cracked software, phishing or download mirrors. Download Nexdir only from the official links in #resources.

**5. Protect private information.** Do not post your own or anyone else's personal data. Remove names, paths and screenshots that show private files before posting.

**6. Never share credentials.** Never post passwords, tokens, license keys or recovery codes — the team will never ask for them.

**7. Use the right channel.** Questions go to #support, bugs to #bug-reports, ideas to #feature-requests. One topic per forum post.

**8. Report bugs responsibly.** Include version and steps to reproduce. Do not upload private files to reproduce a problem.

**9. Report security issues privately.** Never post vulnerabilities publicly. Use GitHub Security Advisories: {links.security}

**10. Follow the team's instructions.** Moderators may remove content or members that break these rules. If you disagree with a decision, contact the team calmly in a DM.

Thank you for helping keep this community friendly and safe! 💙
